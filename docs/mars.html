HOW TO PLAY & EXPLORE MARS IN 4 SIMPLE STEPS

🚜 1. MOVE YOUR ROVER
• Click ANY tile on the map to drive there! The rover will automatically plot the route.
• Or use your keyboard: W A S D or ↑ ← ↓ →.
• Flat terrain costs 5 Energy. Sand dunes and craters cost 20-25 Energy.
🔬 2. TARGET SCIENCE SITES
• Look for glowing gold beacons: ★ SCIENCE SITES.
• Drive onto or near them, then click:
  📷 [1] Mastcam Photo (10 MB, +30 SP)
  🔬 [2] Laser APXS Scan (20 MB, +60 SP)
  🔩 [3] Drill Sediment Core (25 MB, +110 SP)!
📡 3. BEAM DATA TO EARTH (DSN)
• Your internal memory holds 100 MB.
• When storage gets full, click TRANSMIT TO EARTH (or press [T]).
• Data empties into the Earth DSN ground archive and converts into permanent Research Points (RP) and Funding!
⚡ 4. RECHARGE & UPGRADE
• If battery runs low, press [SPACE] to deploy solar panels for +30 Energy.
• Visit the Rover Workshop to unlock the MMRTG Nuclear Generator (infinite power, immune to dust storms)!
• Click COMPLETE ROVER MISSION when finished!
